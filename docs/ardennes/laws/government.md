# Constitutional & Government Law

This code governs the institutions and public offices of Ardennes.

## GC-001 — Organization of the Commonwealth

### §1. National Authority
The Commonwealth government exercises national authority through the High Chancellor, Grand Hierophant, Commonwealth Assembly, High Court, ministries, and other lawfully established offices.

### §2. Regional Administration
Regional governments administer local affairs subject to national law.

### §3. Public Offices
Additional ministries, commissions, and offices may be established as required for administration of the Commonwealth.
