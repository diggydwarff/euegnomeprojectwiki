# Civil & Commercial Law

## CC-001 — Foreign Commercial Establishment Act

### §1. Foreign Businesses
Non-citizens and foreign nationals may establish shops, cafés, restaurants, and other lawful commercial establishments within Ardennes, subject to national and local regulations.

### §2. Jurisdiction
Foreign-owned businesses operating within Ardennes remain subject to Ardennian law while operating inside Commonwealth territory.

### §3. Property and Development
Commercial construction must comply with applicable land-use, architectural, environmental, and regional rules.

### §4. National Treatment
Permission to conduct business does not itself grant citizenship, political office, territorial rights, or exemption from Ardennian law.
