# Environmental & Protected Lands Law

## EC-001 — Protected Lands and Habitat Act

### §1. Protected Areas
The Commonwealth may designate national parks, protected habitats, ecological reserves, and other conservation areas.

### §2. Development
Construction, extraction, clearing, and industrial activity may be restricted or prohibited within protected areas.

### §3. Wildlife
Wildlife and rare species within protected areas may receive additional protection from unnecessary killing, capture, or habitat destruction.

### §4. Caelorian Protected Lands
Designated portions of the Caelorian Archipelago are protected for ecological, scenic, and scientific value.
