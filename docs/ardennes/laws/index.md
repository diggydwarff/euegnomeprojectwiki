# Legal Code of Ardennes

This section contains enacted laws of the Holy Commonwealth of Ardennes.

## Code Structure

### Constitutional & Government Law
Rules governing national institutions, public authority, regional administration, and official offices.

### Civil & Commercial Law
Rules governing property, businesses, foreign commercial activity, contracts, and ordinary civic matters.

### Agricultural & Animal Welfare Law
Rules governing farming, livestock, animal welfare, and agricultural practices.

### Environmental & Protected Lands Law
Rules governing national parks, protected habitats, conservation areas, wildlife, and restricted development.

## Citation Format

Laws should use a short code and sequential number, for example:

- **AC-001** — Agricultural Code, Act 001
- **CC-001** — Civil & Commercial Code, Act 001
- **EC-001** — Environmental Code, Act 001
- **GC-001** — Government Code, Act 001

Individual sections within an act may be cited as **§1, §2, §3**, and so on.
