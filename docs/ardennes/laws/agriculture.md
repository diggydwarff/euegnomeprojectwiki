# Agricultural & Animal Welfare Law

## AC-001 — Iron Golem Farming Prohibition Act

### §1. Prohibition
The construction, operation, or maintenance of farms designed to repeatedly spawn and kill iron golems for industrial resource production is prohibited within the Commonwealth.

### §2. Scope
This prohibition applies to public, private, commercial, and state-operated facilities.

## AC-002 — Animal Welfare and Livestock Husbandry Act

### §1. General Standard
Animals kept for agriculture must be provided sufficient usable space, shelter where appropriate, and conditions that do not rely on severe overcrowding or prolonged confinement.

### §2. Animal Classes
For enclosure standards, animals are grouped into **large animals** and **small animals**.

**Large animals** include cattle, horses, donkeys, llamas, camels, and comparable animals.  
**Small animals** include sheep, goats, pigs, chickens, rabbits, and comparable animals.

### §3. Minimum Space — Large Animals

| Number of animals | Minimum enclosure |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | 6×6 blocks |
| 3–4 | 12×12 blocks |
| 5–8 | 18×18 blocks |
| 9–12 | 24×24 blocks |
| 13–16 | 30×30 blocks |
| Each additional 4 | +6 blocks to each dimension |

### §4. Minimum Space — Small Animals

| Number of animals | Minimum enclosure |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | 4×4 blocks |
| 3–4 | 6×6 blocks |
| 5–8 | 10×10 blocks |
| 9–12 | 14×14 blocks |
| 13–16 | 18×18 blocks |
| Each additional 4 | +4 blocks to each dimension |

### §5. Usable Space
Buildings, machinery, inaccessible landscaping, or other areas unavailable to the animals do not count toward the minimum enclosure requirement.

### §6. Breeding and Holding
Animals may not be mass-bred or stored in conditions that would violate the normal space and welfare requirements of this act.

### §7. Transport and Temporary Holding
Temporary holding areas may be smaller than permanent enclosures only where the confinement is brief and necessary for transport, sale, veterinary handling, or relocation.

### §8. Humane Slaughter
Methods relying on prolonged suffocation, drowning, burning, entity cramming, or comparable unnecessary suffering are prohibited.

## AC-003 — Companion and Working Animal Protection Act

### §1. Protected Animals
Tamed, named, companion, and working animals are protected from deliberate neglect, abandonment, and unnecessary killing.

### §2. Working Animals
Working animals must be provided appropriate resting space when not in active use.

### §3. Abandonment
Owners should make reasonable arrangements for animals they no longer intend to keep rather than leaving them permanently confined or unattended.
