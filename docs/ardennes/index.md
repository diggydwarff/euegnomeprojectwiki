## Overview

The Holy Commonwealth of Ardennes is a sovereign nation organized around a strong central government, regional administration, national religious tradition, private enterprise, and public stewardship of strategic resources and protected lands.

Ardennes emphasizes orderly development, engineering, agriculture, cultural continuity, conservation, and national defense.
