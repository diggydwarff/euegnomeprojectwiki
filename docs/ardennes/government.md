# Government of Ardennes

Ardennes is a **constitutional religious commonwealth** with authority divided between national leadership, the judiciary, ministries, and regional governments.

## National Leadership

### High Chancellor
The High Chancellor is head of state, chief executive, and commander of the armed forces. The office directs national policy, administration, foreign affairs, and defense.

### Grand Hierophant
The Grand Hierophant is the highest spiritual authority of the Commonwealth and guardian of Sacred Doctrine. The office advises on matters involving national religious tradition, civic life, and institutions of faith.

## Commonwealth Assembly

The Commonwealth Assembly provides regional representation and a formal forum for governors to advise on legislation, development, and national policy.

## High Court

The High Court is the highest judicial body of Ardennes. It interprets national law, resolves major disputes, and reviews questions involving the legal code.

## Ministries

The Commonwealth may maintain ministries for areas including:

- Defense
- Engineering
- Agriculture
- Infrastructure
- Industry and Commerce
- Energy and Resources
- Foreign Affairs
- Culture
- Land Preservation

## Regional Government

Regions are administered by governors. Regional governments may manage local development and administration, but remain subject to national law and the authority of the Commonwealth government.
