# Geography of Ardennes

Ardennes contains a mixture of settled heartlands, protected interior territory, forests, agricultural districts, coastlines, islands, volcanic landscapes, and colder northern environments.

## Ardennian Heartlands

The principal population and administrative center of the Commonwealth. It contains the national capital and major urban development.

## Lower Ardennes

A developed southern region with settlements, agriculture, transport links, and commercial activity.

## Ardennian Interior

A largely protected inland region reserved for national parks, wilderness, conservation, and controlled development.

## Caelorian Archipelago

An island region centered on **Caelora**. The archipelago includes tropical and volcanic landscapes, protected habitats, and smaller surrounding islands.

Parts of the archipelago fall under national-park and protected-habitat rules.
